# S08 validation protocol (declared before official execution)

The fixed matrix is S08 × normal/heavy/peak × seeds 1–10 × static/dynamic: 60 trials, 30 matched pairs. Configuration is `experiments/configs/s08-validation.json`. No per-seed timing, route, threshold or demand adjustments are permitted. Preserve all outcomes, including ERROR, TIMEOUT and TELEPORTED. The S05/S07 120-trial dataset is frozen and is not regenerated by this work. The 560-run suite is explicitly out of scope.

## Fixed implementation

Use the already merged 3×4 S08 research network, existing emergency candidate routes plus the northern bypass, H1 at J9, ambulance0 at 300 s, E3 accident vehicle at 300 s, E7 blocker departure at 307 s and E11 blocker departure at 315 s. Horizon 900 s, step 1 s. Existing policy evaluates each eligible step, uses a 10-second commitment and at least max(5 s, 15%) ETA benefit; observed blocked active routes bypass the commitment. Subhashini's implementation and Argh's unmerged work are unchanged.

Instrumentation does not select routes, tune scores or add extra applications. A scorer wrapper returns exactly the original scorer's values and captures actual calls. Additional scoring of the active route is labelled audit work and is outside planner computation timing. Recorded candidate evaluations count actual Replanner scorer calls, excluding dispatch and audit-only scoring; dispatch evaluations are retained separately.

## Evidence and metrics

Every S08 decision preserves time, physical edge, speed, distance since departure, full SUMO route before application, active route suffix, complete planner road snapshot, reachable candidates, actual candidate scores, active-route score, predicted benefit, threshold, elapsed commitment, invalidity, reason and gate result. Applied decisions additionally preserve the exact TraCI command, completion time, full route read back, route index and remaining route suffix. SUMO may retain already-travelled prefix edges in getRoute(); compare the remaining suffix to the candidate. Never count a setRoute request alone as success.

The two environment-change metrics are observational: for E7 and E11, latch the first newly blocked/non-finite observation or travel-time increase at least max(5 s, 15%) relative to dispatch. These labels are monitored under both strategies and do not control decisions. First/second refer to chronological detection order, which can differ from vehicle departure times. Missing observations remain blank. Incident insertion, full physical stop, meaningful deterioration, candidate eligibility and route application are distinct times.

CSV fields retain the established names: ambulance_dispatch_time, ambulance_arrival_time, actual_travel_time, successful_route_changes and replan_checks. Added fields include candidate_evaluations, first/second_environment_change_time and first/second_applied_replan_time/latency. All decision and application timestamps remain in events.json. Successful travel/arrival fields are blank for every non-success. Static never invokes the Replanner and has zero post-dispatch CityBrain route changes.

Existing result checkpoints are validated, never rerun because an outcome is unfavorable. Resume rejects changed revision, inputs, malformed schemas or missing/tampered evidence. Analysis reads raw files without modifying them. After documentation/results are committed, resume the original dataset only from the generating revision in a separate checkout; do not rewrite original provenance.

## Predeclared interpretation

Report the dynamic 0/1/2+ applied-replan distribution, including unknown counts if ERROR interrupts a trial. Do not demand exactly two. Repeated multi-replan evidence requires at least two distinct seeds and at least two demand profiles with audited two-or-more applied replans. Otherwise classify as implementation demonstration only. This fixed synthetic scenario alone will not be labelled strong synthetic evidence: timing/topology sensitivity, further controls and calibration remain absent.

Report success/teleport/other-failure counts and rates, successful-trip mean/median/SD, mean route changes and per-applied-replan latency. Paired travel differences are conditional on both strategies arriving; read alongside full failure rates. Charts must expose failed/teleported counts and clearly label successful-trip-only metrics. Do not generalize to real traffic or emergency response. S06 remains the unreachable-destination boundary case, and hardware remains future work.

## Evidence serialization correction before v2

The complete v1 matrix remains preserved at `experiments/results/s08-official-v1`, generated by `b7842f4bf553586cf1e13015b3289fef82ed6970`. Its 60 outcomes are 30 dynamic SUCCESS and 30 static TELEPORTED. Publication was stopped because post-run resume/audit found that the generic JSON sanitizer converted infinite ETA/score/threshold values to null, making the saved numeric comparison unavailable. No v1 trial is deleted or relabelled.

V2 preserves infinite S08 evidence values as the explicit JSON strings `Infinity` / `-Infinity`; null remains unavailable, NaN is rejected. Both in-memory and serialized evidence are checked. Infinite-benefit applications must have an infinite old ETA and finite candidate ETA. This preserves the existing policy's infinite-cost comparison, rather than changing its decisions. Network, demand, incidents, candidate routes and policy are unchanged. Tests cover the saved-evidence round trip. Per the provenance rule, v2 is a separate full matrix from a new clean generating commit; its results are never pooled with v1.

V2 also defers rewriting an aggregate during checkpoint-only resume until the complete audit passes. A regression test tampers with the final checkpoint input and verifies the original aggregate remains unchanged. V1’s interrupted resume aggregate was restored from its 60 intact result checkpoints, without changing or rerunning any trial.
